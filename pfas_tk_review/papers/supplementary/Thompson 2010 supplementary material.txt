SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL


S1: Detailed description of data sources used in calibration of Vd

A human specific volume of distribution (Vd) was calibrated according to equation 2c (in article). This equation requires knowledge of serum concentrations (CP), a daily absorbed dose (DP) and elimination rate constant (kP). The data used to calibrate the Vd value came from a site near the Dupont Washington Works Facility located adjacent to the Ohio River, about 10 miles from Parkersburg, West Virginia, USA. Specifically, data was obtained from two communities with drinking water provided by either the Little Hocking Water Association or the Lubeck Public Service District.
