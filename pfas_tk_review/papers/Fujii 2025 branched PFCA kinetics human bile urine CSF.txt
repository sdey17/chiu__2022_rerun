ABSTRACT (verbatim)
"Perfluoroalkyl carboxylic acids (PFCAs) are persistent in the environment, and can enter
the human body. This study aimed to investigate the in vivo kinetics of branched PFCAs with
seven to fourteen carbon atoms (C7 to C14) using samples collected different individuals:
bile (n = 5), urine (n = 10), and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) (n = 7), with their
corresponding serum. This study revealed that the clearance values of PFCAs were greatly
affected by whether they were linear or branched. Furthermore, it demonstrated that C7 and
C8 branched PFCAs had higher total (renal plus fecal) clearance values than their linear
counterparts and that they were more easily excreted from the body via urine (higher renal
clearances). However, when the chain length was C9 or longer, the clearance value was almost
the same as that of linear PFCAs because fecal clearance through the bile is the main route
of clearance from C9 onwards, and there is no significant difference in fecal clearance
between branched and linear ones. The ratio of branched to linear PFCAs in the CSF was
similar as that in the serum for all PFCAs (C7 to C14) measured, and there appeared to be no
difference in blood-brain barrier permeability. This information will be useful for
evaluating potential health risks related to branched PFCAs."

WHAT IS NEW HERE AND NOWHERE ELSE
  - Human BILE concentrations of branched AND linear PFCAs across C7 to C14, with paired
    serum. This is the only human dataset that resolves BILIARY clearance by isomer, and
    only the second human bile PFAS dataset at all (after Harada 2007, n=4, PFOA/PFOS only,
    and Fujii 2015, linear PFCAs only).
  - It supplies the mechanistic reconciliation of the isomer literature: branched PFCAs
    clear faster than linear ONLY where urine is the dominant route (C7-C8); once biliary/
    fecal clearance takes over (C9 and longer) the isomer difference vanishes.
  - CSF/serum branched:linear ratio equals the serum ratio for C7-C14, i.e. no isomer
    selectivity at the blood-brain barrier.
  - It is the only human source of ANY kinetic parameter for branched PFTrDA (C13) and
    branched PFTeDA (C14).

LIMITS: n = 5 bile, 10 urine, 7 CSF, and these are DIFFERENT individuals for each matrix.
The authors call it "a preliminary investigation". No half-life is reported; only clearances.
Numeric clearance values are in the paywalled tables and were NOT obtained.
