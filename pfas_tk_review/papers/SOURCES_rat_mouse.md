## Search strategy record

PubMed/NCBI E-utilities esearch (not the MCP wrapper, which ANDs all terms and
over-constrains) with these queries, each returning the counts shown:

- `(perfluoro*[tiab] OR PFOA OR PFOS OR PFHxS OR PFNA OR PFBS OR PFBA OR PFHxA) AND (half-life OR halflife OR toxicokinetic* OR pharmacokinetic*) AND (rat OR rats OR mice OR mouse)` → 200 hits, all triaged by title+abstract.
- `(perfluoro* OR PFOA OR PFOS) AND (Oat1 OR Oat3 OR Oatp OR "organic anion transport" OR Urat1 OR Npt2 OR OATP1A1 OR Slco OR Mrp OR "renal transporter")` → 42 hits.
- `Kudo N[au] AND (perfluoro* OR fluorinated)` → 20 hits. `Lau C[au] AND perfluoro* AND (pharmacokinetic* OR half-life OR disposition)` → 7 hits.
- `Buist SC[au]` → 6 hits. `Klaassen CD[au] AND (Oatp OR Slco) AND (mouse OR mice) AND (gender OR sex)` → 3 hits. `Ljubojevic M[au] AND Oat2` → 4 hits. `Hanhijarvi H[au] AND perfluoro*` → 6 hits.
- Europe PMC via MCP for the Zurlinden 2025 anchor and for `perfluorooctanoate renal transporter rat Oat Oatp sex difference`.
- Firecrawl scrape (proxy "auto") for the two ASPET papers whose abstracts were otherwise unavailable in full.

## Gaps and failures that matter

1. **Sex-specific mouse PFOA half-lives were never obtained.** Lou et al. 2009 is the *only* mouse PFOA dataset in Zurlinden 2025, and neither its abstract, nor EPA 2024 PFOA §3.3.2.1, nor ATSDR Table 3-5 (which has no PFOA–mouse rows at all) reports male and female mouse PFOA half-lives separately. The EPA re-analysis values (mouse M 25.6 d, F 21.5 d) therefore cannot be checked against a published measured pair.
2. **Kemper 2003 and Kirkpatrick 2005 are unpublished reports.** Much of the rat PFOA and PFHxA half-life record rests on them, and they could only be read through ATSDR's tabulation.
3. **Katakura 2007 is not in PubMed**, so no quantitative Npt2 (Slc34a1) or Mrp2 (Abcc2) PFAS kinetics were obtained from any source.
4. **Ljubojević 2004** (rat Oat1/Oat3 castration/testosterone) was not retrieved; it is used only as cited.
5. **Chang 2009** (gestational/lactational K⁺PFOS in rats) was not retrieved; no numbers extracted.
6. **No strain-matched rat-vs-mouse PFOA experiment exists in the retrieved literature.** Every rat/mouse comparison is a cross-study comparison, except Tatum-Gibbs 2011 (PFNA), Chang 2008 (PFBA), Chang 2012 (PFOS), Sundström 2012 (PFHxS), Gannon 2011 (PFHxA) and Iwai 2011 (PFHxA), which did run both species in one laboratory.
7. **No study has measured renal Oatp1a1 protein in rat and mouse side by side**, nor tested PFAS in an Slco1a1-null mouse. This is the single most consequential missing experiment.
