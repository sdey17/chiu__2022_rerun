Overview of Risk Reduction Approaches for PFAS

The Netherlands addresses certain PFASs under EU regulation. For these initiatives see European Union actions listed under Countries’ resources on PFAS.
Within the context of REACH, the EU regulation on chemicals management, the Netherlands together with Germany, Denmark, Norway and Sweden agreed early 2020 to prepare a joint REACH
restriction proposal to limit the risk to the environment and human health from the manufacture and use of a wide range of PFASs.
In 2025 the five member states finalized the background document. ECHA prepares an opinion on the restriction proposal. EC will prepare a decision for the EU Member States to vote on. After
adoption and scrutinised by the European Parliament and Council, the restriction is expected to enter into force in 2027.
Since 2024, all PFAS according to the OECD definition, are placed on the 'List of Chemicals for Priority Action' of the OSPAR convention, which means direct action and emission reduction, to air
and water, is required. Therefore in NL all PFAS are placed on ZZS list, ZZS Navigator | Risico's van stoffen. This results in these PFAS emissions to be reported.
